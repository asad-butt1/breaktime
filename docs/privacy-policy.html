BreakTime Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: BreakTime works entirely on your phone. We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Nothing you do in BreakTime is sent to us or anyone else.

Who we are

BreakTime is an Android app that helps you take breaks from distracting apps. It is developed by Asad (“we”, “us”).

What the app accesses, and why

  • Accessibility Service. To lock the apps you choose, BreakTime uses Android's Accessibility Service API. It only reads the package name of the app that comes to the front of the screen (for example, com.instagram.android), and only so it can show the break screen over an app you chose to lock while a break you started is running. It does not read screen content, messages, passwords or anything you type. Nothing it sees is stored or sent anywhere.
  • List of installed apps. BreakTime shows the apps on your phone so you can pick which ones to lock. This list is read on your device and never leaves it.
  • Notifications. Used to show your break countdown and to tell you when your apps unlock.

Data stored on your phone

BreakTime saves the apps you chose to lock, your current break and your break history (dates, lengths and whether each break was completed) so it can show your streak and progress. This information is stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Data we collect

None. BreakTime has no account, no analytics and no servers. We do not collect, sell or share personal or sensitive data.

Advertising and purchases

Future versions may show an ad when you start a break (free plan only) and offer an optional Premium subscription through Google Play. If ads are added, they will be provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers as described in Google's advertising policy; this policy will be updated before that happens. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

Children

BreakTime is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
